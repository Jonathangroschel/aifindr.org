Saturalabs accepts MP3, WAV, MP4 and MOV recordings up to 50 MiB. Users describe
one audible sound, then compare Isolated sound with Background audio, the remaining
mix. Separated WAV downloads require an account and sufficient paid credits.
Results depend on the recording; this is not a guarantee of clean separation or
reconstruction of missing words.

Separate free browser utilities support tasks such as trimming, joining, channel
selection, format inspection and audio-file size calculations without an account
or credits. These local utilities are distinct from the uploaded AI workflow.
